# ADR-044 — The tourist tax, when collected online, is charged to the hotel's own payment account

**Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-09-30); nothing to build until a payment provider is live · **Date:** 2026-09-30
**Depends on:** ADR-010 (Stripe behind a payment adapter), ADR-020 (collection left to its own record) · **Supersedes:** nothing

## Triggering event

ADR-020 put the imposta's computation and declaration outside the fiscal gate, but left open whether
the platform **collects** it: "that needs its own record in WP1.4, before any code". WP1.4 shipped
without collection. The payment provider is being set up (ADR-010), so the question needs an answer
before the checkout is built.

## Context

- Since DL 34/2020 art. 180 the hotel is *responsabile d'imposta*: it owes the tax to the comune
  whether or not the guest paid. A wrong or missing declaration costs 100–200% of the tax due.
- Whoever holds the money carries that exposure. Binding rule 6 and D11 keep BookOne out of anything
  fiscal, and ADR-011 allows no fiscal-adjacent agent tools.
- Three options:
  - **(a) Never collect.** BookOne computes the amount; the hotel charges it at the desk. Today's
    behaviour: the booking flow shows the tax as a note (`booking/quote.ts`).
  - **(b) Collect on the hotel's own account.** The tax is a separate line of the stay's payment,
    charged into the hotel's own Stripe account (Connect Standard). The money never passes through
    BookOne. The hotel pays the comune and stays the responsible party.
  - **(c) BookOne collects and pays the comune.** BookOne becomes part of a tax chain.

## Decision

**(b) is the target, and (a) stays until a payment provider is live.** Guardrails:

- The amount always comes from the engine for a comune with a verified rules file. It is never typed
  by hand and never generated by a model (binding rule 7).
- The tax is its own line, never folded into the room price, and it moves with the stay's payment
  (charged when the balance is charged).
- **Agents never compute, change, waive or refund the tax.** A refund is money: a human approves it
  under the router's hard rules.
- Online collection is a per-property flag, off by default (ADR-019).
- A comune without a verified rules file is never charged online. The note stays.
- BookOne gives the hotel the ledger (ADR-043) and a draft of the declaration. The hotel files it.

**(c) is rejected**, not deferred.

A change complies if no code path moves tourist-tax money into an account that is not the
property's own, and no agent tool can touch the tax.

## Cost of change / cost of not changing

**If wrong:** (b) is additive over (a): switching the flag off returns to the note. Moving to (c)
later would need this record superseded and a fiscal review.

**If not done:** whoever builds the checkout decides, under deadline, whether a tax amount goes into
the `payments` ledger, which is exactly the drift rule 6 exists to stop.

## Alternatives rejected

- **(c) BookOne as collector.** It puts BookOne in the tax chain and collides with D11 and rule 6.
  Rejected outright.
- **(a) forever.** Guests pay twice (online, then at the desk), and the hotel reconciles cash by
  hand. It stays only as the interim.

## Consequences

- Until Stripe is live nothing changes: the booking flow keeps the note.
- The checkout work (ADR-010) includes the tax line, the flag and the reconciliation against the
  ledger in its scope.
